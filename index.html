THE CHALLENGE

Reconciliation should not depend on spreadsheets and guesswork.

Bank transactions, invoice exports, and payment references often arrive in different formats. Tracking down partial payments, missing references, or unexpected amounts turns routine close work into manual investigation.

01 / MANUAL COMPARISON

Too many records to cross-check.

Teams compare bank activity with open invoices, often across multiple spreadsheets and client systems.

02 / EXCEPTIONS

Not every payment fits neatly.

Overpayments, partial payments, fees, and unclear memos require extra research and judgment.

03 / REVIEW

Context is hard to reconstruct.

Without clear supporting evidence, reviewers spend more time understanding why a match was proposed.

THE FINCLINE APPROACH

Rules match. AI explains.
You confirm.

Technology should accelerate the review process without taking financial judgment away from the accountant.

01 · RULES ENGINE

Deterministic matching

Evaluates payments and invoices using defined matching signals, including invoice references and amount relationships. The rules engine calculates scores and variances.

02 · FINCLINE AGENT

Plain-English explanations

Turns the rules engine's supplied evidence into a clear explanation of why a match was suggested — without inventing signals, scoring, or financial calculations.

03 · YOUR ACCOUNTING TEAM

Human review and approval

Review suggested matches, investigate exceptions, and explicitly confirm each decision before moving results into the reconciliation workflow.

“Rules identify the match. Your team makes the decision.”
